## Summary
This source is the primary text capture of the functional-programming article and gives the fullest view of its structure and examples. ## Key Claims
- Pure functions, declarative style, recursion, and immutability make PowerShell easier to reason about
- PowerShell's native pipeline helpers already behave like map, filter, and sort higher-order functions
- Functional error-handling patterns can reduce reliance on broad imperative control flow
